Questions

What's the catch?

Fair question. Straight answers.

Why would you work for free?

We only get paid when a draft turns into a finished project, so every draft has to be good enough to sell itself. The free v0 is our sales pitch. It replaces the proposals, decks, and discovery invoices other shops charge you to sit through.

What does a free draft actually include?

Something real. For a website, working pages you can click through. For an app, live screens of the core flow. For a video, a rough cut of the opening. Enough that you can judge the direction with your own eyes instead of imagining it from a document.

What happens if I don't like it?

Tell us, and either we take one more free swing in a different direction or we part ways. There is no invoice, no kill fee, and no pressure. The draft stays ours; your idea stays yours.

How much does the finished version cost?

It depends on the project, and you'll know the exact number before committing. Every quote is fixed and in writing. Because our overhead is a small team with modern tools, the number is consistently well under agency rates for the same scope.

Who owns the finished work?

You do. Source files, code, accounts, footage, everything. No hosting lock-in and no licensing scheme that rents you your own website.
