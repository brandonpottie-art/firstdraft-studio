Questions

What's the catch?

Why would you work for free?+

We only get paid when a draft turns into a finished project, so every draft has to be good enough to sell itself. The free draft replaces the proposals, decks, and discovery invoices other shops charge you to sit through.

What does the free draft actually include?+

Something real. Working pages with your menu, prices, hours, photos, and reviews already in them, plus three social posts made for you, which are yours to use whatever you decide. Enough that you can judge it with your own eyes instead of imagining it from a document.

What happens if I don't like it?+

Tell us. Either we take one more free swing in a different direction, or we part ways. There is no invoice, no kill fee, and no pressure. The draft stays ours; your business stays yours.

How much does the finished version cost?+

The numbers are further down this page, under Pricing. Every quote is one fixed figure in writing before we start, shaped to the job, so a site that needs less costs less. Because we run a small studio with modern tools and no account managers, the number is consistently well under agency rates for the same work.

What does it cost to keep it running?+

Very little. Your domain name is about $15 a year. Hosting for a site like the ones above is free. Email at your own domain can be free too. We set it all up in your name so nothing depends on us.

Am I tied into anything?+

No contract and no minimum term. The build is one fixed price, quoted in writing before we start. If you take a care plan afterwards it is month to month, and you can stop it whenever you like.
